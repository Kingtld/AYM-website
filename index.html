A Youth Movement for real relationships & encounters with God

Seeking God together as young people can feel like a big step. Because what if your earnest pursuit is met with silence? At AYM, we create space for real relationships and real encounters with the living God, where you'll feel His presence and walk away transformed through the power of His Spirit.

Why Apostolic Youth Movement

We are built on three foundations that guide everything we do.

Faith

Deepening our relationship with God through prayer, worship, and the study of His Word. Faith isn't just for Sundays - it transforms every day of our lives.

Fellowship

Building authentic relationships where everyone belongs. Don't worry about having it all together - pull up a chair with us and just be you.

Service

Partnering together to invest in our community. We are called to be the hands and feet of Jesus, serving our neighbors and transforming our valley.

Prayer & Support

No struggle is too hopeless for Jesus!

If you're going through a difficult time or need someone to pray with you, you're not alone. Our community is here to support you through every season of life. We want to be there for you as others have been there for us.
