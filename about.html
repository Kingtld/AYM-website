About Apostolic Youth Movement

O MOHAU WA MODIMO

Our Vision

To be a nationally recognised youth movement that raises spiritually grounded, educated, and community-conscious leaders who are equipped to transform lives across every province of South Africa.

AYM envisions a South Africa where every young person, regardless of background or circumstance, has the spiritual foundation, education, and opportunity to rise to their fullest potential and lead meaningful change in their communities.

Our Mission

The Apostolic Youth Movement is committed to the holistic development of young people through spiritual formation, academic encouragement, and compassionate community service. Through regular conferences, outreach programmes, skills development initiatives, and recreational activities, AYM creates safe and inspiring spaces where youth are empowered to grow in faith, pursue education, and actively contribute to the upliftment of those around them.

Our mission is carried out across three key pillars:

Spiritual Growth

Through conferences, prayer services, and fellowship with senior pastors and bishops held three times a year.

Education & Opportunity

Through career expos, career days, academic encouragement, and creating pathways for youth still in school.

Community Service & Outreach

Through second-hand clothing donations, sanitary towel contributions to schools, gala dinners, hiking, and family day events.

Our Core Values

Our identity is built on five cornerstone values that guide every gathering, programme, and relationship.

Faith

Rooted in prayer, worship, and spiritual fellowship with God and one another.

Education

Equipping youth with knowledge as a powerful tool for personal and communal transformation.

Service

Giving back through donations, sanitary dignity, outreach, and compassionate care.

Community

Building belonging and connection through shared events, conferences, and experiences.

Growth

Expanding our reach, impact, and opportunity — from Gauteng to every province of South Africa.

Get Involved

Whether you are looking for spiritual growth, educational opportunities, or a community that cares, there is a place for you at AYM. We meet regularly across Gauteng's 12 branches and welcome every young person ready to grow in faith and purpose.
